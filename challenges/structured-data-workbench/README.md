# Structured Data Workbench

This maintained React demo consolidates four rule-driven data challenges into
one workspace:

- Salsify contributes its original product datastore, dynamic property and
  operator controls, value selection, filtering behavior, and result table.
- Stormtech and Zygo contribute their equivalent four-book fixtures, ordered
  comparison rules, documented presets, and explicit null and empty outcomes.
- Vue contributes its exact five-driver fixture, individual and select-all
  controls, emitted record IDs, and All, Selected, and Unselected filters.

Product filtering, book sorting, and driver selection remain distinct
workspaces beneath shared navigation. All records are deterministic and local,
and the historical Express and MongoDB services are not contacted. The
immutable sources remain under their four original challenge directories.

The canonical route is `structured-data-workbench`. The former
`product-data-table`, `configurable-book-sorting`, and
`formula-one-driver-explorer` routes, together with all four historical source
slugs, remain available as redirects.
